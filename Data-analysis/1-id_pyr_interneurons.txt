Identify pyramidal cells and interneurons (using awake trials)

Only consider neurons that are spiking 'enough' (more than 75 spikes in entire trial)

Use first moment and spike width to id pyramidal cells and interneurons (from Muessig et al., 2019):
       - Pyramidal cells: spike width >= 300 ms and first moment <= 25 ms
       - Interneurons: spike width < 300 ms and first moment > 25 ms

Spike width already have. Need to compute first moment.

Computing FIRST MOMENT:
1) Get spike train from spike times
2) Compute autocorrelation up to a lag of 50ms. This ensures we don't include local maximums due to theta cycles, which have a time scale of 100ms.
3) Compute first moment (mean lag weighted by autocorrelation, excluding t=0)


How to interpret results?
- sharp peak of autocorrelation in beginning: probably bursting -> weighted mean lag will be a small value.
- peak in beginning that decays slowly: probably not bursting -> weighted mean lag will be bigger.

Extra notes:
TEMPORAL AUTOCORRELOGRAM: It represents the spike timing relative to each spike across the spike train. Useful to get info about refractory period, burst tendency, rhythmic firing,...
