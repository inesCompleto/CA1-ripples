During sleep trials

1) Select which EEG channel to use to search for ripple events:
      - Bandpass filter EEG signal (100-250 Hz)
      - Compute power envelope of filtered signals
      - Choose channel with highest power standard deviation

2) Detect ripple events on selected channel
      - Compute threshold to id ripples as the 99th percentile of power of selected channel
      - Find peaks on selected channel where power is above threshold for at least 5 ms
      - find beginning and end of ripple event by finding extremes of peak where power drops below mean+std  

3) Detect slow-wave sleep epochs (where ripples are more likely to be happening):
      - Compute mean speed during sleep trials. Consider epochs where mean speed < 2.5cm/s
      - Compute theta/delta ratio:
         Select channel with highest delta power
      - Find epochs where mean speed < 2.5 cm/s and TD ratio < 2

4) Keep ripples events ‘inside’ sws epochs
